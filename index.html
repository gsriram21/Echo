Generate the caption layer from any film

Four stages, running here in the browser and a serverless function. The audio is analysed for onsets, loudness, duration and texture. Two frames per sound are shown to Claude, which names the sound and — the interesting bit — decides whether the picture already shows what makes it. If it does, the caption is dropped. A caption that says what the shot already says is noise.

Any mp4, mov, webm — audio only works too.

Proxied through the serverless function — browsers cannot fetch YouTube video streams directly for CORS reasons. Short clips work best.

Load a clip first.
